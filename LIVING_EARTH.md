# Living Earth — implementation record

Status: implemented; production build and functional browser review complete. User visual acceptance, current-build performance/soak testing and Radeon 780M verification remain open.

## Accepted direction

Worldwide reconstructed low-flight scenery with real geography; substantial buildings and forests, physical ground materials, coordinated lighting, improved mapped airports and a type-specific live aircraft fleet. Existing clouds and Neon are preserved. Free assets/data only. Airport aircraft remain exclusively live. Desktop target 60 fps; Radeon 780M target 30 fps at 1080p output and at least 75% internal resolution. Wait for starting-area detail; explicit degraded entry on failure.

## Preservation and evidence

The pre-existing dirty tree and September 15 build receipt are copied into `.graphics-review/living-earth/baseline/`. The previous production review on port 3052 is retained. New output uses an isolated build directory and a separate port.

## Implementation ledger

- **Worldwide mapped coverage.** The worker retains accepted mapped building bodies beyond the detailed-building cap. The cap now limits architectural embellishment. Successful empty tiles are distinguished from unavailable data. Worker protocol is 22 at all six consumers.
- **Regional architecture.** Eight building families include attached housing and airport terminals. Explicit map tags take precedence. Natural Earth country geometry supplies coarse regional palettes and roof proportions, including Bali and Japan. Convex mid-rise towers can receive bounded setbacks, while courtyard footprints remain intact. Relief includes plinths, projecting ledges and facade divisions, bounded to 160 prominent buildings per tile; the larger roof-variety budget and every mapped body remain intact.
- **Ground materials.** Classified near terrain uses physical surface colors and metre-scale material detail. Imagery remains the distant and unclassified fallback. Developed-area classification permits pavement treatment without allowing trees or clutter into those pixels.
- **Forest coverage.** Stable geographic woodland cells create continuous areas of 3D canopy. Nearby crowns have geometric curvature, trunks, broken leaf edges and filtered leaf-scale relief; distant crowns use cheaper geometry on the same occupied land. Rotated, irregular stands replace the first review's visible plantation grid. Quarter-tile render groups keep the detailed, shadow-casting crowns close to the aircraft without changing source positions or removing stands. Terrain support is required, including valid negative elevations. Births and refinements are eased in place.
- **Conservative settlement infill.** Inferred houses require mapped roads plus residential land or observed built-up cover. Candidate clearances reject water, woods, crops, existing structures, airports and unknown land. Roadside homes follow road direction. They are scenery, not asserted real addresses.
- **Mapped airports and roads.** Terrain-supported pavement follows mapped polygons and centerlines. Source widths are used when supplied; fallback widths and markings are explicitly inferred. Runway/taxiway markings and night edge lights improve airport readability. Synthetic car activity is excluded from known airfields. No parked aircraft, service fleets or flight events are fabricated.
- **Live fleet.** Nineteen original aircraft-family models have near and matching distant representations, cockpit/cabin windows, engine inlets, gear, propellers/rotors and navigation lights. Type codes select the family; unknown types retain the legacy fallback. Source track identity and motion remain unchanged. These are representative families, not exact liveries or subvariants. The player fleet is unchanged by this pass.
- **Arrival readiness.** Satellite entry waits for nearby terrain, materials, building/road tiles, forests, pavement and compiled models/shaders. Source geography resolves across the local ring; DEM-supported scenery must be ready in the visible arrival area. Behind-camera pending work is reported separately because three-tile does not refine that terrain until it enters view. A fresh-boot deadlock found during review is covered by normal/reversed-depth and rebase tests. After 45 seconds, the player can retry or explicitly enter with reduced detail. There is no automatic timeout reveal. Flight is held while loading. Neon retains its previous arrival behavior.
- **Streaming cost.** Pavement reuses terrain queries at coincident vertices and uploads only changed elevations and newly admitted triangles. Its readiness census runs at 10 Hz and invalidates immediately for tile changes. Forest instances upload only changed matrix ranges; nearby geometry uses 342 triangles per stand, including trunks, with unchanged occupancy. Nearest-first support queues use per-tile repair cadence so every tile can make progress. Immutable building-run counts are cached. CPU profiling identified pavement readiness scans and repeated building-stat walks as leading avoidable costs. Fast-flight review found a 302.11 MiB texture peak against the unchanged 300 MiB budget. Terrain retention fell from 120 to 104 MiB, which passed the continuous-flight run; a subsequent 96 MiB configuration still peaked at 301.78 MiB on a far arrival. The current 80 MiB retention setting reserves more arrival headroom, but its full allocation run remains pending.
- **Measurement.** `?graphicsReview=1&graphicsMemory=1` lazily installs the existing GL allocation observer before Canvas creation. Ordinary flight does not load that diagnostic chunk. Texture allocations, renderbuffers and unique geometry buffers are reported separately. Allocation wrappers are excluded from the separate frame-pacing run.
- **Lighting feedback revision.** During the playable preview, the user identified lighting and atmosphere as the weakest area. The clear-sky background had ignored overcast even while direct sunlight was reduced by 83%. A weather-driven sky palette now follows the same damped state, with a deeper clear zenith, a solar aureole and an overcast/fog transition. Daylight fill is reduced and shaded material response is slightly cooler; night lighting endpoints remain unchanged. The existing depth pass now integrates atmospheric density along a physical eye-to-surface ray, correcting Mercator distance and keeping the closest 250 metres clear. Distant low terrain loses contrast before high ridges; reported fog thickens the same law. No new render target or pass was added. Cloud density, shape, motion and raymarch lighting are unchanged by this revision. The daylight material cache suffix advances to `v2`.

The current clouds, atmosphere interactions and Neon implementation are preserved. Regional context and inferred architecture remain separate from authoritative source geography. See `public/data/LIVING_EARTH_PROVENANCE.md` and `CREDITS.md` for data and artwork provenance.

## Acceptance

Technical checks do not establish visual acceptance. Fixed-pose screenshots inspect appearance; they are not evidence of moving-flight stability. Continuous flight checks must leave terrain and the governor unpinned and measure actual travel. The RTX 5080 cannot certify the Radeon 780M target. User visual acceptance, the full geographic/day/night matrix and any unexecuted soak remain explicitly pending until their evidence is recorded.

The world remains a reconstruction from free map and land-cover data. Missing geometry cannot become surveyed truth through inference. This pass adds substantial coverage and physical structure; it does not claim photogrammetry or a finished AAA art standard.
