The source is a photogrammetric scan of a nineteenth-century bronze model,
not an on-site scan of Liberty Island. Skyloom's modifications: weld and
simplify the sculpture to three detail levels; place the 46.05 m figure on a
46.95 m authored foundation/pedestal; add eleven-point Fort Wood, stepped
stone courses, colonnade, windows and parapets; replace bronze with varied
verdigris copper and a gold flame; bake separate stone/copper/gold material
and night illumination attributes. No original texture is redistributed.
Pedestal and processing code: Skyloom, MIT.

Files: monument-liberty-far-v2.glb, monument-liberty-medium-v2.glb,
monument-liberty-high-v2.glb. Builder: scripts/build-liberty-detail.mjs.
The assets are 93 m tall; the game applies a separate, explicitly stylized
1.4× visibility boost in addition to its existing landmark scale.

Dimensions reference: National Park Service, Statue Statistics,
