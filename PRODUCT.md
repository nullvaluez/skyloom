# Skyloom

Skyloom is a browser flight game built around a live satellite Earth and real
air traffic. Players enter free flight or takeoff and landing activities,
choose an aircraft, explore destinations, and encounter other aircraft.

## Approved visual extension — 2026-11-01

Continue the incumbent Three.js / React Three Fiber world and existing UI.
The approved direction is cinematic realism: coherent physical scale,
believable mapped architecture, readable aerial photography at distance,
organic vegetation nearby, and recognizable geological silhouettes.

The first regional showcases are Manhattan and Yosemite Valley. Improve the
shared material, terrain and scenery systems; reuse existing landmark assets
and streaming. Do not introduce another terrain overlay or engine migration.

Use free assets and tools with recorded provenance and redistribution rights.
Prefer CC0 materials/models and public-domain elevation. Existing provider
terms and ordinary hosting/bandwidth costs still apply; free assets do not
mean unlimited free map delivery.

Browser access and iPhone remain product constraints. The performance target
is 60 fps, with the existing device tiers, governor and budgets retained.
Desktop phone emulation does not establish Safari behavior or thermal
performance on a physical phone.

The owner's 2026-11-01 instruction, “Push to main and enable everything,”
authorizes `MATERIAL_SCANS`, `SCENERY_FORMS`, `SHOWCASE_EARTH`,
`BUILDING_REALISM`, `CITY_HORIZON`, and `TWILIGHT_BALANCE` to default on.
A plain Enhanced Satellite session uses them without URL flags. This release
decision does not certify the open High resource-budget gate or physical-iPhone
behavior. The implementation record, licenses, review evidence and remaining
checks are in [FLY_PREMIUM_EARTH.md](FLY_PREMIUM_EARTH.md).

This is an approved extension of an existing world, not a new visual identity.
No concept/FORM roll or generated composition was performed or approved.
There was no pre-existing DESIGN.md to replace. No claim of AAA completion or
physical-device certification is implied by this implementation.
